**Yusuf Okonkwo-Vieira**
b. 1980, a border town between two countries that no longer exist. Lives and works between Mexico City and Brooklyn.

*Rehearsal for the Broken Back*, 2025
Reading room / social-practice presentation
Usb cables, lead, dried seaweed and clay bricks from a demolished factory
Dimensions variable; site-specific

Courtesy of the artist and Galerie Nachtigall, Berlin. Commissioned by La Biennale di Venezia with the support of the Sharjah Art Foundation.
