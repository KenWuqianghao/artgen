Yusuf Okonkwo-Vieira (b. 1980, a border town between two countries that no longer exist) lives and works between Mexico City and Brooklyn. Their practice spans reading room / social-practice presentation, cooking and forms of collective listening. Recent presentations include the Kochi-Muziris Biennale and the Sharjah Biennial. They were a resident at Villa Romana, Florence and are represented by Galerie Nachtigall, Berlin.
