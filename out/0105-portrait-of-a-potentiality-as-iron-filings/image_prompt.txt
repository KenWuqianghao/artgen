a dark gallery with one very large floor-to-ceiling video projection; on the screens: a choir singing a shipping forecast, an underwater shot of mosquito netting sinking, an empty swimming pool at dusk; screens glow and light the concrete floor; a few silhouetted visitors sit on the floor. Located in an abandoned cinema near Rialto, Venice Biennale. The work evokes cosmotechnics and loom as computer. installation view at dusk, blue hour light through arched windows, a single silhouetted visitor for scale, atmospheric. No text, no captions, no watermark.
