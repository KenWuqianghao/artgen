**Bao Mensah**
b. 1980, a mission station in the Kimberley. Lives and works between Kochi and Manila.

*Against Opacity*, 2025
Textile installation (hand-sewn patchwork banners)
Blue tarpaulin, plastic bottles and clay bricks from a demolished factory
Dimensions variable
