*Against Rupture* intimately negotiates the vegetal rupture of the shared meal. Working deliberately with beeswax, hotel bed linen and reclaimed tin roofing, Minor Gardens Working Group asks what it might mean to negotiate the shared meal not as an object of knowledge but as a futurity to be lived with.

Beeswax, hotel bed linen and reclaimed tin roofing are half-buried in indigo. The beeswax is not a metaphor. It is beeswax.

The work draws on Gayatri Chakravorty Spivak's notion of the subaltern and Saidiya Hartman's account of critical fabulation, while refusing the ancestral closure that either might imply. Between the shared meal and the hum, a third term emerges, one that makes visible rather than names.

Rather than offering resolution, the work holds open a space of rupture in which the viewer is invited to sit with what cannot be resolved. It is, finally, a proposition about the commons: porous, and therefore necessary.
