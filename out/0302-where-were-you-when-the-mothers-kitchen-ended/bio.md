Ngozi Ionescu (b. 1983, a village whose name no longer appears on maps) lives and works between Tbilisi and Beirut. Their practice spans performance for the camera, writing and the reactivation of neglected archives. Recent presentations include the Istanbul Biennial and the Liverpool Biennial. They were a resident at Künstlerhaus Bethanien, Berlin and are represented by Galería Umbral, Mexico City.
