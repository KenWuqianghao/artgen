a dark gallery with one very large floor-to-ceiling video projection; on the screens: someone sweeping the sea with a broom, hands passing a stone from one person to the next, a burning piano on a frozen lake; screens glow and light the concrete floor; a few silhouetted visitors sit on the floor. Located in an abandoned cinema near Rialto, Venice Biennale. The work evokes surveillance and the algorithmic gaze and predictive violence. installation view, dim gallery, single dramatic spotlight, deep shadows, cinematic, medium format film, desaturated. No text, no captions, no watermark.
