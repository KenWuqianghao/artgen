a dark gallery with 9 large video screens of different sizes at different heights; on the screens: archival footage of a border crossing, slowed 400%, a child sleeping on a pile of plastic bottles, close-up of soil from the artist's grandmother's village being burned; screens glow and light the concrete floor; a few silhouetted visitors sit on the floor. Located in a former boatyard on Giudecca, Venice Biennale. The work evokes translation and untranslatability and mother tongue. installation view at dusk, blue hour light through arched windows, a single silhouetted visitor for scale, atmospheric. No text, no captions, no watermark.
