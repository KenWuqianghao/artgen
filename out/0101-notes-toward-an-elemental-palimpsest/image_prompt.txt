a dark gallery with one very large floor-to-ceiling video projection; on the screens: a drone shot of a salt flat, a glacier, upside down, a machine translation of a lullaby, read aloud by a synthetic voice; screens glow and light the concrete floor; a few silhouetted visitors sit on the floor. Located in the Gaggiandre docks, Venice Biennale. The work evokes plant intelligence and vegetal time. installation view, wide-angle, exhibition documentation photography, natural light from high windows, concrete floor, muted palette, 35mm, slight film grain. No text, no captions, no watermark.
