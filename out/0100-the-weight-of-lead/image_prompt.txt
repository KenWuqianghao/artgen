a dark gallery with one very large floor-to-ceiling video projection; on the screens: a machine translation of a lullaby, read aloud by a synthetic voice, the artist's mother peeling fruit in silence, a child sleeping on a pile of charcoal; screens glow and light the concrete floor; a few silhouetted visitors sit on the floor. Located in a squero (gondola workshop) in San Trovaso, Venice Biennale. The work evokes the garden as resistance and creole garden. installation view, dim gallery, single dramatic spotlight, deep shadows, cinematic, medium format film, desaturated. No text, no captions, no watermark.
